# Voice lessons

Read before every draft. Target length: about 50 lines. Consolidate every ~10 letters.

## How this file works

- After he returns his final letter, compare it to the draft. Log each change with the reason.
- Tag each lesson with its scope: **[general]** (every letter) or **[posting]** (this role or employer only).
- A change becomes a general rule only after it shows up **twice**. Until then it sits under Candidates. This stops one-off preferences from turning into over-corrections.
- If one of his edits weakens the letter, say so before logging it. Log it as his preference with a note, not as a rule to follow blindly.
- Never log a fact here. Facts live in `facts.yaml`.

## General rules (seen 2+ times, or standing instructions)

1. Confidence states facts. Cut any sentence whose only job is self-commentary.
2. Do not use the same strong word twice in one letter.
3. Informal and authentic over corporate polish. It should sound like him, edited, not first-pass.
4. No em dashes. No neat-bow closing paragraph. No "it's not X, it's Y".
5. Treat his edits as senior. Critique them honestly when warranted.
